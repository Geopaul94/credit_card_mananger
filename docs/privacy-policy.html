Card Vault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Card Vault ("the app") is a personal card organizer published by Ghonestapps. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: your data stays on your device, encrypted; we run no servers and receive nothing.

Data the app stores

The app lets you save details of your payment cards: card number, holder name, expiry date, bank and card name, an optional payment due day, and optional notes. The app deliberately does not collect or store CVV/CVC codes or PINs.

All card data is stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is protected by your device's secure keystore. We — the developers — have no server and no access to any of your data.

Biometric lock

The app is locked behind your device's fingerprint, face, or screen-lock credential. Biometric verification is performed entirely by Android; the app never sees, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

Camera (card scanning)

You can optionally photograph a card to fill in its details automatically. Text recognition runs on your device via Google ML Kit. The photo is processed in memory and deleted immediately after recognition — it is never stored or uploaded by the app.

Google Drive backup (optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google account to back up your card data. The backup is an AES-256-encrypted file saved to your own Google Drive's hidden app-data area. Only this app on your account can access it, we cannot read it, and it is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or can be removed via your Google account settings. If you never enable backup, the app performs no network activity with your card data.

Notifications

If you set a payment due day, the app schedules local reminders on your device. These are generated locally; no data leaves your phone.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no registration, no server of ours
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No selling, sharing, or transmitting of your personal data to anyone
  • No CVV/PIN storage of any kind

Data deletion

Delete any card in the app at any time, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling it. If you used Drive backup, the encrypted backup is removed automatically on uninstall, or manually via Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and stores no data about any identified person other than what you choose to enter.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Card Vault keeps your credit, debit and prepaid card details in one place —
encrypted on your phone, locked behind your fingerprint, and never sent to
anyone. No account, no sign-up, no servers.

WHY CARD VAULT
Most of us keep card details in a notes app or a photo in the gallery. Card
Vault gives them a proper home: AES-256 encrypted, behind your device lock,
and organised so you can find what you need in seconds.

EVERYTHING ON YOUR PHONE
• AES-256 encryption, with the key held in your device's secure keystore
• Fingerprint, face, or PIN required to open the app
• No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics
• We run no servers — your data is never uploaded to us

SCAN A CARD INSTEAD OF TYPING
Point your camera at a card and Card Vault reads the number, expiry, holder
name and bank for you. Recognition happens entirely on your device and the
photo is deleted the moment it is read.

NEVER MISS A BILL
Set the day your bill is due and get reminders 3 days before, 2 days before,
the day before, and on the day itself. Mark a bill paid with a swipe and the
next cycle starts fresh.

OPTIONAL SECURITY CODE
You can save a card's CVV if you want to — it is entirely optional. It stays
encrypted with the rest of the card, is hidden by default, and asks for your
fingerprint or PIN every single time you view or copy it, then hides itself
again automatically.

PRIVATE NOTES
Keep bank login IDs, customer-care numbers or reminders alongside each card,
encrypted just the same.

ENCRYPTED GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP (OPTIONAL)
Changing phones? Back up to your own Google Drive as an encrypted file that
only this app on your account can open. Turn it off and the app makes no
network requests with your card data at all.

BUILT FOR PEACE OF MIND
Card Vault cannot make payments, and it never shares your card details with any
app, service or person. It is a private place to keep what you already own.

DATA SAFETY — declare honestly in Play Console
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As of v1.0.2 the app CAN store a CVV (optional, user-entered, on-device only).
Make sure the Data Safety form and the privacy policy both reflect this before
uploading. Key points to declare:
  • Data is collected on-device only; nothing is transmitted to the developer
  • Financial info (payment card details) — stored on device, user-provided
  • Optional Google Drive backup is user-initiated and encrypted
  • Data is encrypted at rest; the user can delete any card at any time

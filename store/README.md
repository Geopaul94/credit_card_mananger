# Play Store listing assets

Text for the Play Console listing lives in `listing-text.txt`.

Screenshots and the feature graphic are **not yet in the repo** — they currently
exist only in Play Console. Export them and add them here:

    store/
      listing-text.txt
      feature-graphic.png     1024 x 500
      screenshots/            phone screenshots (min 2)

The signing keystore, its passwords, and released .aab files are deliberately
NOT here — they live in `D:\PlayStoreBackups\cardvault_drive_playstore_backup`
and must never be committed.
